## 💡 Image Optimization Tips
* **WebP Format:** If possible, export your new images in **`.webp`** format for maximum page load speeds.
* **Resolution:** Keep header banners at least **1920x1080px**, and general content images around **1200x800px** or **800x600px**.
* **Lossless Compression:** Run images through tools like *TinyJPG* or *TinyPNG* to keep file sizes small without losing any visible quality.
