Testimonials

Minslake is a family home in Rotonda, South West Florida, near Cape Haze, Boca Grande and Englewood on the Gulf of Mexico. It offers over 2,400 sq. ft of living space, and the pool looks out onto a large picturesque lake. This relaxing area is the perfect spot for a laid-back, quiet vacation.

Steve and Grace Keeling, August 2016

It certainly can’t be bettered for relaxation by the pool over a water view.

Read the full review

Rotonda has a wide choice of villas. Minslake should be one of your top choices. We enjoyed our second visit in July 2016 and heartily recommend the villa. It certainly can’t be bettered for relaxation by the pool over a water view.

We’d returned to the Rotonda in 2013 and just lucked upon Minslake when previous villas were not available. Now we’d always come back to Minslake. It has a helpful owner who uses and cares for the property and a location that is so peaceful and scenic.

The villa itself has a light airy feel, at its best in the TV area. The patio doors look through the pool area and then on to the lake and the green in the distance. The pool area is totally private with no hint of being overlooked. The neighbouring villas are hardly visible and only glimpses of a distant roof in the distance across the water. The owner’s description is spot on. We arrived after she’d left a fortnight earlier. The villa was immaculate. It had an “as new” feel with everything well cared for. Turned out the carpets had been replaced just a few weeks earlier and it looked freshly painted.

Much thought has gone into the detail. The provided PC has a printer with paper and ink. Good for the boarding passes before driving to Orlando on our last day. Good how to use TV / DVD instructions with a decent package of programmes. The two bikes are great for exploring the Rotonda (early morning before the day heats up) Google maps on my mobile (cell phone) was great for never getting lost and finding the best route and cut-throughs on the “spoke” roads The beach parasol and chairs were great quality and perfect for our trips to Gasparilla Island / Boca Grande.

Our favourite beach by the old Boca Grande lighthouse was 30 mins drive away, but there are closer ones at Englewood and further up Manasota Key. Oh, and do stop for the ice cream at the Loose Caboose on Gasparilla.
We’ll be back

Steve and Grace Keeling, August 2016

Mr & Mrs Keeling - August 2013

If you go to the Rotonda you won't go wrong staying in Minslake.

Read the full review
This was our first return to the Rotonda since 2004 after escaping Hurricane Charlie by just 24 hours. This time we were blown away by the villa location and its quality interior. We'd searched for a lake view. We had that view in spades and it was all to ourselves. The neighbouring villa was set back and not visible from the pool area and the double lot meant nothing but trees to the right. It was an idyllic private setting to laze an afternoon away in the sun. The interior was as good. It was well maintained, light, airy, designed to maximise the lake view and had comfortable beds and furnishings. We knew the Rotonda well, and this was up there with best villa we'd stayed in, and way above the average ones. It stood out by being someone's home and not just a rental income. Extras provided were great. The bikes were good for sightseeing the Rotonda ... but only early morning !!! Too hot later. Detailed directions made TV and DVD use idiot proof, or at least we could switch between them without asking our son and technical advisor back in the UK. A 30 minute trip, driveway to beach, returned us to our favourite beach on Gasparilla Island and just under 20 minutes took us to the closest beach at Engelwood. North from Engelwood up Manasota Key took us to a couple of good beaches with facilities, both well worth a visit, but we kept returning to Gasparilla Island. The parasols, beach chairs and cooler provided made those trips easy and comfortable. To celebrate our last day we ended up being one of the first cars over the new Gasparilla Bridge and were snapped on the contractor's camera. If you go that way get your camera ready for the Ospreys fishing from the telephone poles alongside the road. If you go to the Rotonda you won't go wrong staying in Minslake.

​Mr & Mrs Keeling - August 2013
