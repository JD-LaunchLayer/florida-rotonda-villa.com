House Rules & Booking Terms

Please review these guidelines before submitting a reservation request.

  • Check-in 4:00 PM
  • Check-out 10:00 AM
  • Occupancy Maximum 8 guests
  • Smoking No smoking / no vaping indoors
  • Pets No pets allowed
  • Age Primary guest must be at least 25 years old
  • Quiet hours 10:00 PM – 8:00 AM

Deposit: A 30% deposit is required to secure your reservation. The remaining balance is due 30 days before check-in.

Security deposit: A refundable damage deposit of $500 is held and returned within 7 days of departure, subject to property inspection.

Cancellation:

  • 60+ days before arrival: full refund of deposit minus a $75 administration fee
  • 30–59 days before arrival: 50% of the total stay is forfeited
  • Fewer than 30 days before arrival: no refund

Sample policy for this demo only — final terms appear on the official T&Cs page.
