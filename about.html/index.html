This family home is furnished to the highest standard and offers over 2,400 sq. ft living space. It comprises of the Master Bedroom, which has an en-suite with Jacuzzi bath, walk-in wardrobe and 2 further bedrooms with bathroom, a large family room, a formal living room and dining area.This house benefits from its own electrically heated pool (charged separately). Although pool heat is not absolutely essential most of the year it can on occasion, prove to be most welcome during the sometimes cooler winter months. The pool looks out onto a large picturesque lake which attracts a whole host of wildlife to the doorstep.

Rotonda, South West Florida is located near beautiful Cape Haze, Boca Grande & Englewood on the Gulf of Mexico. This relaxing area is the perfect spot for enjoying a laid back, quiet vacation. There are outdoor activities for every adventurer. You can jet ski, parasail, fish or charter a boat from one of the area’s marinas. There are numerous golf courses and nearby there are many beautiful beaches all within 10-15 minutes.

Pine Valley has the newest golf course on Rotonda, opened in January 2008. This is rumoured to be one of the best golf courses in the Charlotte County area and Minslake is ideally located to this as you can view it from across the lake at the same time as wild life of otter, osprey and many others are often seen on Rotonda whilst in the inter-coastal you will nearly always see dolphin and even occasionally a manatee. For the nearby beaches on Boca Grande and Minnesota Cay, as well as the beaches on Palm Island and Don Pedro Island which are only accessible by boat. Boat rental is easy and very popular. A Shopping Mall is only 10 minutes, with two massive shopping Outlets Ellenton and Miromar only a 40 minute drive away. There is a host of fantastic restaurants all very local. We particularly recommend The Morning Muse Cafe for breakfast. Rotonda has its own park, with tennis and children's play area just 5 minutes from home.

For the comfort and safety of all guests there is no smoking permitted within the villa.
